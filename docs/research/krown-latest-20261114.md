The strongest examples of useful structure are TSLA (separate support and reversal thresholds), HYPE (pending daily-close condition), CRSP (range, breakout and invalidation), RDW (explicit correction of candle timeframe), and BTC (opposite positions across distinct horizons). The ambiguous BE, ZTS, unknown final ticker and several numeric caption errors are useful rejection cases.

No forecast has been tested here against future prices. No price-derived entry, target or stop was added to repair missing source instructions. No model confidence should be interpreted as a probability of trade profit.
